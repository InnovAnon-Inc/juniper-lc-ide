we wanna be able to print our bubble notation programs. we need two sizes:

- one size renders the expressions as 1 inch radius circular diagrams

- another size renders the expressions large enough that the smallest bubbles are 1 inch. that is, the bubbles representing the variables to be substituted are large enough that the first size can be overlayed into it, physically performing the substitution
